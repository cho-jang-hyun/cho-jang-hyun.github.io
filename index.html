Janghyun Cho, Jimmy Chiun, Guillaume Sartoretti , Changjoo Nam
Submitted, 2026

A decentralized budget-aware exploration framework for communication-free teams with directional sensing. Rather than exchanging maps, goals, or messages, each robot coordinates through its incidental observations.
